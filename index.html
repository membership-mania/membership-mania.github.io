How scoring works

Each region's total is its membership count. The bar shows how far up the ladder those members go: the light bar is everyone attending at least the entry level, the middle bar the middle tier or higher, the bright bar the top tier. The engagement score gives each member points for the highest level they reach: 5 for the top tier, 3 for the middle tier and 1 for the entry level. A region's score is the points it earns out of the maximum (every member at the top tier), from 0 to 100.
